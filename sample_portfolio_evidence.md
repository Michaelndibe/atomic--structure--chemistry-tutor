# Sample Portfolio Evidence

## Example 1 — Atom Builder

Input:
- Protons: 8
- Neutrons: 10
- Electrons: 10

Expected output:
- Atomic number: 8
- Mass number: 18
- Net charge: -2
- Interpretation: negative ion

## Example 2 — Quiz

Question:
An atom has 11 protons and 12 neutrons. What is its mass number?

Correct answer:
23

Reason:
Mass number = protons + neutrons = 11 + 12 = 23.

## Example 3 — Short-answer evaluation

Question:
What is atomic number?

Student answer:
"Atomic number is the number of protons in the nucleus."

Expected evaluation:
Strong answer.

## Example 4 — Incomplete answer

Student answer:
"Atomic number is related to the atom."

Expected evaluation:
Needs improvement. The answer should explicitly state that atomic number is the number of protons.
